01 / THE IDEA

Technology that works with farmers.

🌾

The Problem

Traditional farming can require time, physical effort and careful monitoring of field conditions. Small farmers need affordable technology that is practical, simple and useful.

🤖

Our Solution

AGRO-BOT combines sensors, motors, an Arduino controller and Bluetooth communication to create a compact smart-farming prototype.

02 / WORKING PRINCIPLE

From sensor data to action.

01

Sense

Soil, light, temperature and distance sensors collect information from the surroundings.

02

Process

The Arduino reads sensor values and controls the robot according to programmed conditions.

03

Decide

Based on the conditions, the system can activate functions such as watering and movement.

04

Act

Motors, relay and servo mechanisms perform the required farming task.
